RD SK

Research Dashboard: a free, open-source Android app for keeping track of academic paper submissions.

Download the app (APK, version 3.5) Source code Privacy policy

Android 7 or newer. After downloading, open the file and allow “Install unknown apps” for your browser when Android asks. Updates install over the old version and keep your data.

What it does

Every submission in one placePapers, journals, dates, decisions and how long each one has been under review.
Your own Google SheetSign in with Google and RD SK keeps a master sheet in your Drive, synced both ways. Reinstall and just sign in again.
Journal logins and remindersOne tap opens each journal's submission site; reminders when it is time to chase the editor.
ABDC ratings and suggestionsBuilt-in ABDC 2025 list and suggested next journals after a rejection.
Ask AIJournal recommendations, fit checks and cover letters for conceptual, review and empirical papers, with your own free Gemini key.
Email import (optional)A helper script in your own sheet reads journal decision emails and updates statuses.

Your data stays yours

RD SK has no server and no tracking. Your records live on your phone and, if you sign in, in a sheet in your own Google Drive. RD SK can only access the files it creates. Read the privacy policy.
