RD SK Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026 (updated for co-author sharing)

In short: RD SK keeps your research submission records on your phone and, if you sign in with Google, in a Google Sheet in your own Google Drive. The developer runs no server and never receives your data. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What RD SK is

RD SK is a free, open-source Android app for tracking academic paper submissions: papers, journals, submission dates, decisions and related notes. The source code is public at github.com/faltu9522/Research-Dashboard.

Data stored on your phone

Everything you enter (papers, titles, authors, journals, dates, statuses, notes, manuscript IDs, journal login links, editorial-office emails, saved AI answers and settings) is stored in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it from the phone.

Google sign-in and Google Drive

If you choose Sign in with Google, RD SK asks for one permission:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file: access only to the files RD SK creates (or files you explicitly open with it). RD SK cannot see, read or change any other file in your Google Drive.

With this permission RD SK creates one Google Sheet (“RD SK Master”) in your Drive and keeps it in sync with the app, using the Google Sheets and Google Drive APIs directly from your phone. The sheet belongs to you; you can open, edit, share or delete it in Google Drive like any other file. RD SK uses your Google account's email address only to show which account is signed in.

Limited Use. RD SK's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the features you see in the app. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not transferred to third parties except as described below at your request, and not read by any person.

Sharing with co-authors (optional)

If you share with a co-author, RD SK creates a separate Google Sheet in your Drive containing only the papers that list them as an author, and gives their Google account edit access to it (Google emails them). The co-author's RD SK opens it once through Google's file picker, which is what lets their app use that one file. Co-author email addresses you type are kept on your phone and in your own sheet's hidden settings. Stopping sharing deletes the shared sheet.

Optional features that contact other services

These run only when you use them, and send only what the feature needs, directly from your phone (or your own Google account) to that service:

  • Ask AI (Google Gemini): if you add your own Gemini API key, your question is sent to Google's Gemini API. The question includes the selected paper's title, approach, notes and submission history. On Google's free tier, Google may use this content to improve its products. Your key is stored on your phone and in your sheet's hidden settings tab.
  • Ask in Gemini app: the question is copied to your clipboard and passed to the Gemini app on your phone. That app's own privacy terms apply.
  • Journal checks (Crossref): journal names are sent to the public Crossref API to see what the journals published recently. No personal data is sent.
  • Journal websites: the in-app browser opens journal submission sites. You sign in to them yourself, and Google Password Manager may fill in your details. RD SK does not store your journal passwords.
  • Email import and daily insights (optional helper script): if you install the RD SK helper script in your own sheet, it runs inside your own Google account (Google Apps Script). It reads journal-related emails in that account's Gmail to update submission statuses, and writes daily research insights from Crossref (and Gemini or Claude if you set a key). It runs under your authorization; the developer has no access to it or to your email.
  • GitHub sync (optional): if you add a GitHub token, RD SK saves a backup of your data to the GitHub repository you choose.

What the developer receives

Nothing. RD SK has no backend server, no accounts with the developer, no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking libraries.

Removing your data and access

  • Phone: uninstall RD SK or clear its storage in Android settings.
  • Google Drive: delete the “RD SK Master” sheet.
  • Google access: remove RD SK at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or tap Sign out in the app.
  • Helper script: in the sheet, open Extensions → Apps Script and delete the project, or turn off its triggers from the RD SK menu.

Children

RD SK is a tool for researchers and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
